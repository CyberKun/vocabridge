# Code of conduct

vocabridge is a technical project that welcomes contributors of every background and level of experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers; everyone started somewhere.
- Credit other people's work and sources.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer at YOUR-CONTACT-EMAIL. Reports will be handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove content or block participants who break this code.
